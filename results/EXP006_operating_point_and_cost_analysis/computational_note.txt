EXP006 Computational Characterization
=====================================

This analysis reports workstation-measured timing and serialized Random Forest model footprint. It is not a hardware benchmark and does not establish compatibility with any specific microcontroller or wearable device.

Measurements:
- total feature-extraction wall-clock time
- outer-fold Random Forest training time
- batch inference time on outer-test epochs
- approximate single-epoch predict_proba time after warm-up
- serialized model size
- Random Forest tree-node counts

Environment:
macOS-26.6.2-arm64-arm-64bit-Mach-O
